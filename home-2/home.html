Material Stories

Fabrics With Character

01 — Linen

Natural Texture, Relaxed Elegance

Linen breathes with the room. Its irregular weave catches light unevenly, giving every fold a quiet, handcrafted character that never feels stiff.

02 — Velvet

Rich Texture, Sophisticated Depth

Velvet holds shadow and light in the same fold. Dense, warm and substantial — it's the fabric of choice for rooms designed to feel indulgent.

03 — Sheer

Soft Daylight, Visual Openness

Sheers diffuse rather than block. They keep a room connected to the outdoors while gently veiling the view — light without glare.

04 — Blackout

Privacy, Comfort & Controlled Light

Engineered layers block light completely, built for bedrooms, media rooms and anywhere rest and focus come first.
