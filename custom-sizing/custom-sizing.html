Measurement Guide

Understand How We Measure

Curious what our measuring team looks at? Here's a plain-English guide — no tape measure required before your visit.

Measured left to right at three points — top, middle and bottom of the window or recess — using the narrowest measurement to guarantee a clean fit.

Taken top to bottom at the left, centre and right of the opening, or down to your chosen drop point for curtains.

For a floor-length look, we measure from the track or rod down to just above the floor, allowing for skirting boards and airflow.

For blinds fitted inside the window frame, we measure the recess depth and width at multiple points, since older frames are rarely perfectly square.

Choose inside mount for a clean, built-in look, or outside mount to cover the full frame and make windows appear larger. We'll recommend the best fit for your space.

You don't need to measure a thing before your visit — this guide is just so you know what to expect. Our team brings the tools and does the precise measuring for you.
