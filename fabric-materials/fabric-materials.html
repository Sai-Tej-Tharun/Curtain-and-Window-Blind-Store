Fabric Categories

Seven Materials, Every Kind of Light

Linen

Relaxed, breathable weave with natural texture and a soft drape.

Cotton

Crisp and even-textured, easy to keep clean and holds colour well.

Velvet

Dense, plush pile with a soft sheen — heavy drape, strong insulation.

Polyester

Practical and colourfast, resists fading and holds its shape well.

Sheer

Voile-like and translucent, softens daylight without blocking it.

Blackout

Tightly woven layer that blocks nearly all light and outside noise.

Textured Weaves

Dimensional, nubby weaves that add depth without a bold pattern.

Silk

Smooth, luminous fabric with an elegant drape and refined finish.
