Our Story

Started at a Kitchen Table, Fabric Everywhere

Loom & Light began in 2014, when our founder couldn't find a curtain maker who cared about how light actually moved through a room — only about selling standard widths off a shelf. So she started making them herself: measuring windows by hand, sourcing linen from small mills, sewing late into the evening.

Word spread through a few neighbours before it spread anywhere else. A decade later, that same attention — one window at a time — is still how we work, just with a larger workroom and a few more hands.

Our Philosophy

"A window is the one part of a home that changes all day. We design fabric to work with that, not against it."

— Our approach to every consultation

Craftsmanship

From Raw Fabric to a Finished Window

Every order passes through the same hands, in the same order, whether it's one window or a whole house.

Fabric Selection

We work with a small list of mills chosen for how their linen, cotton and velvet drape and hold colour over time.

Cutting & Pattern

Each panel is cut to your exact measurements, with pattern and weave direction matched across multiples.

Stitching & Finishing

Hems, pleats and headings are sewn by hand-guided machine, then pressed flat before a single seam is checked.

Fitting & Installation

Our installers hang, dress and steam every curtain and blind on site, so it falls the way it was designed to, perfectly.

Our Showroom

See and Feel Every Fabric First

Photos never show how a linen actually falls or how a blackout weave feels between your fingers. Our showroom holds full-size hanging samples of every collection, arranged by light control and colour, so you can decide with the fabric in hand.

Our Process

Five Steps, One Point of Contact

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A short call or showroom visit to talk through light, privacy and the look you're after.

  2. 02

    Measurement

    We measure every window on site — no estimating from photos or floor plans.

  3. 03

    Design

    You choose fabric and hardware; we confirm it before cutting begins.

  4. 04

    Manufacturing

    Your curtains or blinds are cut and sewn to order in our workroom.

  5. 05

    Installation

    A trained installer fits, hangs and dresses everything, and won't leave until it's right.
